## Claim
In the installed aiohttp, `ClientResponse.text()` (or `web.Response.text`) decodes strictly and raises `UnicodeDecodeError` for bytes that are invalid under the resolved charset.

## What it actually does
`ClientResponse.text()` resolves the charset via `get_encoding()` (Content-Type `charset`, UTF-8 for `application/json`/`application/rdap+json`, otherwise the fallback charset resolver, which defaults to `"utf-8"`) and then calls `self._body.decode(encoding, errors=errors)` where `errors` defaults to `"strict"`; invalid bytes therefore raise `UnicodeDecodeError` unless the caller explicitly passes a different `errors` value. `web.Response.text` likewise calls `self._body.decode(self.charset or "utf-8")` with no `errors` argument, and every `Payload.decode` default is `errors="strict"`, so a bytes body invalid under its charset raises `UnicodeDecodeError` there too.
