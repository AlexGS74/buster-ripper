## Claim

## What it actually does
`handle_messages` runs `dump_turn(body)` as the only statement inside `try:` / `except Exception:` (lines 56-60), so any dump failure is logged and cannot escape the handler. The stats DB write on the same path (`update_session_stats` → `db_upsert`) is not a dump/flush call and carries its own `except Exception` in session.py:82-85.
