   requests `monkeypatch`. It snapshots `os.environ`'s keys into a list, keeps

   `monkeypatch.delenv(name, raising=False)` for each (`:99`). Claim 434:
   `delenv` delegates to `delitem(os.environ, name, raising=raising)`
