## The AMBIGUOUS row (detail)

**Row 1 — `T006`'s serve-script wiring has no guard expression, and the two plan-adjacent precedents point at incompatible shapes.**

- **Why the wrong reading is the natural one after this amendment.** The amended `T005` clause is the freshest in-plan wiring precedent: it names the `[[ -n "${DUMP_FORMAT}" ]]` shape inline, and its cross-reference sentence ties `T006` to "this same script on exactly this reasoning". The two forward-visible surfaces cannot both be right; the reader must pick, and the unsafe pick is plan-text-backed.

(2) Invariant conflict: `PI:T006/upstream_tls/verification_on_unless_explicitly_disabled`, `G-007`'s Met-when ("The opt-out defaults OFF"). (3) Expensive: a silent security regression on the local-deployment path the flag exists for, in a surface no VP covers (the plan's own decision — serve-script row is structural, no VP; `US-010`'s harness starts the binary directly). (4) Wrong artifact: the wrong guard *is* the wrong behavior.
