=== DC-S10-C2-r1: what each parser backend hands the handler for two Authorization lines ===
Raw socket, exact wire bytes. The aiohttp CLIENT collapses two Authorization lines
before they reach the wire, which is why the witness needs a socket and not a client.

  C  real-first  -> handler saw ['Bearer sk-real', 'Bearer ']
  C  empty-first -> handler saw ['Bearer ', 'Bearer sk-real']
  PY real-first  -> handler saw ['Bearer sk-real', 'Bearer']
  PY empty-first -> handler saw ['Bearer', 'Bearer sk-real']

The C parser canonicalizes a known name (_http_parser.pyx:98-105 and :382, over the
hdrs-generated table in _headers.pxi) and leaves the value's trailing OWS alone; the
pure-Python parser keeps the peer's spelling verbatim (http_parser.py:184, :235) and
strips OWS. So forward_headers' plain-dict output collapses last-wins on one backend
and not the other. Read against the pre-fix rule that is the two named failures: an
empty bearer arriving second DELETES a real credential (C), and a real credential
first LEAVES the empty bearer forwarded beside it (PY). Observed live while authoring,
before the rule was rewritten: the forwarded mapping held no Authorization at all.
